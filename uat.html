Step-by-Step OdimibO User Guide (How to Select & Scan)

Follow Steps 1 ➔ 2 ➔ 3 ➔ 4 below for 100% reliable scanning

Step 1
Select Game Series, Title & OdimibO Profile

• Quick Switch (Home Tab 🎮): Choose 1. Game Series and 2. Game Title at the top, then tap any character icon in the horizontal strip (or press ◀ Left / Right ▶ on the virtual device).
• Full Database Search (📚 950 DB Tab): Type any game or character name in the search box and tap "📲 Select".

Step 2
Transfer Profile to Hardware or Write to NTAG215 Coin

⚠️ Do NOT tap your phone screen directly on the controller (mobile OSes block NFC Type 2 tag emulation).

🅰️ Using OdimibO BLE / Pixl.js Hardware Emulator:
1) Turn on Bluetooth on your emulator device.
2) On this web app, tap "BLE" (top right) to pair ➔ tap "📡 Send to OdimibO (BLE)".
3) Note: On iPhone/iPad, open this website inside the free Bluefy – Web BLE Browser app.
🅱️ Using Blank NTAG215 NFC Coins / Stickers:
1) Tap "📂 1. Select key_retail.bin" below (one-time setup; saved in browser).
2) Tap "💾 .bin (NFC)" to download the signed 540-byte binary.
3) Write the .bin file to a blank NTAG215 coin using TagMo (Android) or NFC Tools / AmiiBoss (iOS).
Step 3
Activate In-Game Scan Mode & Touch Right Stick (R-Stick)

First, open the NFC scan menu inside your game (or test directly in System Settings ⚙️ ➔ amiibo ➔ Register Owner). When prompted on screen, hold your OdimibO device or NTAG215 coin on the Right Joy-Con Analog Stick (R-Stick) for 2 seconds.

Step 4
Pro Tip: Unlimited Rescans on the Same Day!

Games normally limit each figure to once per day ➔ Simply tap the yellow "🎲 Randomize UID (Rescan)" button and send it to your OdimibO device again. The console will recognize it as a brand-new figure!

Where & When to Scan in Each Game (By Series)

Quick reference for activating NFC scanning across major titles

⚔️ Fire Emblem Series (All Titles)

• Fire Emblem Engage: Reach Chapter 4 to unlock the Somniel ➔ walk to the Gazebo ➔ select "Scan" ➔ scan all 13 Fire Emblem profiles for Fashion Tickets & Music Tickets.
• Fire Emblem: Three Houses: Explore Garreg Mach Monastery and visit the Gazebo ➔ scan to unlock legacy battle music and items in the Holy Tomb.
• Fire Emblem Warriors / Three Hopes: In the Base Camp menu ➔ select System ➔ NFC Scan ➔ claim top-tier weapons & Smithing Stones (up to 5 times/day).
• Fire Emblem Echoes / Fates: In Echoes, open Mila's Turnwheel to summon Illusory Hero allies; in Fates, visit the Crystal Ball in My Castle to recruit heroes.

🍄 Super Mario Series (All Titles)

• Super Mario Odyssey: Hold D-Pad Right (▶) during gameplay and touch R-Stick (grants invincibility, extra hearts, or Wedding outfits), or talk to Uncle amiibo to locate Power Moons!

• Super Mario Bros. Wonder / Galaxy / Donkey Kong Bananza: Press + (Pause Menu) ➔ select the NFC icon and touch R-Stick.

🏹 Zelda: Breath of the Wild & 🗡️ Tears of the Kingdom

1) Enable first: Press + ➔ System ⚙️ ➔ Options ➔ NFC / amiibo ➔ Enable.
2) Hold L to open the ability wheel ➔ select the NFC rune icon.

🔥 2025–2026 Releases (Splatoon 3 / Metroid Prime 4 / Kirby Air Riders / SF6)
